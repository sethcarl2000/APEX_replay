   Cerenkov PMT corrections - RHRS

   Basically to get a 'reconstructed' time (a maximum-likelihood estimate (MLE) of the
   'true' time of intercept of the track with the PMT mirror),
   for a PMT TDC time: $t_{TDC}$ and pmt ADC $adc$, we have: 
   read from pmt 'i_pmt', you want to add:

       t_{MLE} = t_{TDC} - offset[i_pmt] - ADC_corr(adc)

   Where:
       offset[i_pmt] is read from the following table, per-PMT
       ADC_corr(adc) is the value 't_mode', interpolated from the table below (see below for description of t_mode.)

   Skew-normal shift:
   
   The way this PDF is defined is in the following way:
   
       f(t | t0,l,a) =  N((t-t0)/l) * CDF(a * (t-t0)/l)
   
   Where N(.) is a normalized gaus. PDF, and CDF(t) is: int_{-\infty}^{t} N(t') dt'
   The following list is given as: {ADC, t_mode, RMS, t0, l, a}, where:
       ADC     - Cerenkov PMT ADC amplitude for this fit
       t_mode  - the t-point at which the PDF f(t) is maximal. coincides with t0 iff a==0
       RMS     - the variance: <t^2> - <t>^2. Be careful, as this is _not_ centered on t_mode
       t0      - the 'shift' of the distribution. when a=0, this is the gaus. mean
       l       - the 'width' of the distribution. when a=0, this is the gaus. rms
       a       - skewness parameter, this parametrizes how 'skew' the distribution is.
                     when a=0, this is a regular gaus. dist
  ________________________________________________________________________
